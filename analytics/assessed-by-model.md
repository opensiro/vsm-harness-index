**Scope/assumption:** assessments were authored using the regular **ChatGPT Chat browser** surface with the Sol model, as reported by the maintainer. This does not cover ChatGPT Work, Codex, API, Pro/Astra mode, or model overrides.

**Attribution is an estimate, not verified execution provenance.** A model's general availability, rollout date, and an assessment's `reviewed_at` do not prove the exact model selected for any individual execution. Manual legacy-model selection and staggered rollout can affect dates after the boundary. No assessment frontmatter or original reviewer identity is changed.

## Interpretation and boundaries

This is a descriptive grouping for historical analysis, **not** a claim that assessments were executed by, checked by, or independently reviewed by a particular model. It is not a measure of correctness, agent capability, S3* independence, or assessment quality.

To make verified per-assessment `assessed_by` assertions in the future, collect explicit per-run evidence under the proper assessment/reviewer contracts; do not use this date proxy to fill historical frontmatter or a canonical model ledger.
